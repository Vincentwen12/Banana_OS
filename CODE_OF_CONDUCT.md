# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Participation in Banana_OS should be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Take technical criticism gracefully, and give it constructively. This project
  is an engineering exercise, so "this design breaks because X" is welcome;
  "you are an idiot" is not.
- Assume good faith — most disagreements here are about hardware details, not
  about people.
- Respect privacy: don't publish someone else's private information without
  their consent.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language and jokes.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, including unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, commit messages,
and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and issues that
do not follow this code, and will explain the reason when appropriate. Repeated
or serious violations may lead to a temporary or permanent ban from the project.

To report a problem, open an issue or contact the maintainer privately through
their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as
possible.
